Beam Roller – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: Beam Roller has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or location. Your progress is saved on your own device. The Android app uses Google services for leaderboards and ads, and those services collect some data as described below.

This policy covers the Beam Roller game for Android (the “app”) and the web version at jianfengmi.github.io/ballancing. Beam Roller is made by an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

Data saved on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: unlocked levels, stars, medals, best times, high scores, daily challenge results and streak, ghost replays of your best runs, and your settings (controls, sensitivity, sound). This data is never sent to me. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser’s site data deletes it.

Motion sensors

If you choose tilt controls, the game reads your device’s motion sensor to steer the ball. These readings are used only while you play and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Leaderboards (Android app): Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and the times and scores you submit are shared with Google and shown on leaderboards to other players. Leaderboards are optional; you can play without signing in. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Play Games privacy settings.

Advertising (Android app): Google AdMob

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app’s settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases, if offered, are handled entirely by Google Play. I do not receive your payment details.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). It loads the Three.js library from the jsDelivr CDN. The web version has no ads, no leaderboards and no analytics.

Children

Beam Roller is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
